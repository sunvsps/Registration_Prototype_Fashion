## Brand & Style

This design system channels the restrained, cinematic rigor of haute couture runways, legacy Parisian salons, and private invitation-only fashion showcases. The design language marries architectural minimalism with high-fashion editorial discipline. The UI evokes the stillness of an empty runway moments before the house lights dim: precise, unapologetic, exclusive, and strictly curated.

The target audience comprises luxury fashion house executives, atelier directors, show producers, VIP concierges, invited press, and high-profile attendees. The interface must communicate unquestioned prestige, operational precision, and tactile exclusivity.

Visual movements informing the aesthetic:
- **Haute Minimalism:** Deep expanses of midnight obsidian juxtaposed against needle-sharp hairline borders, immaculate negative space, and disciplined column alignments.
- **Editorial High-Contrast:** Large-scale, high-contrast serif headlines paired with ultra-clean, technical grotesque sans-serifs for dense data and seating charts.
- **Refined Materiality:** Subtle metallic gold leaf indicators, satin finishes, and frosted obsidian overlays that emulate physical event invitations, engraved cardstock, and backstage access passes.

## Colors

The core palette is anchored in deep obsidian and burnished champagne gold, supported by warm ivory highlights.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D4AF37`) & Secondary (`#C5A059`):** Burnished champagne and antique gold. Used deliberately for focal moments: RSVP confirmations, front-row (FROW) designations, primary action triggers, and gold foil pill badges. Never applied in broad fills; reserved for metallic accents, fine borders, and active indicators.
- **Neutral Core (`#0B0B0E`):** Midnight obsidian, the default canvas for attendee apps, VIP registration, and showcase presentations. Tonal stepping surfaces include `#141419` (Elevated Panels) and `#1E1E26` (Active States / Floating Modals).
- **Light / Utility Surface (`#FAF9F6`):** Warm crisp ivory. Functions as the high-visibility background mode for backstage credential scanners, logistics check-in stations, and print-ready guest run-of-show manifests. On dark obsidian canvases, this acts as the crisp primary text color.
- **Muted & Supporting Neutrals:** `#70707B` (Muted captioning and disabled borders), `#2A2A36` (Hairline rules and grid dividers), and `#050507` (Deep floor contrast).
- **Semantic Accents:** Status signals avoid loud saturated tones. Confirmed states utilize muted verdigris (`#2E6F5E`), alert/restricted states use vintage rouge (`#8E2A2A`), and waitlisted states utilize muted ochre (`#A37A32`).

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes a deliberate balance between the dramatic cadence of luxury print magazines and the functional precision of real-time seat assignments.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Unapologetically editorial. Used for event titles, designer names, showcase seasons, and welcome greetings. Generous tracking on medium-sized titles; slightly tightened letter-spacing on massive display scales to maintain architectural presence.
- **Functional Interface & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its geometric poise, high legibility, and refined optical balance. It handles guest names, seating tiers, registration inputs, and backstage metrics without competing with the display serif.
- **Editorial Micro-Caps (`label-caps-editorial`):** All uppercase, tracked aggressively (`0.14em`). Used for categorizing badge credentials (e.g., `TIER 1 // FRONT ROW`, `BACKSTAGE ALL-ACCESS`, `PRESS POOL`), timestamps, and table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the architectural layout of runway staging: expansive borders, clear pathways, and razor-sharp structural alignments.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid framed within generous outer margins (`3rem`). Content sections can stretch across wide widths or nest inside structured columns flanked by intentional blank space. Column gutters (`1.5rem`) are defined by subtle 1px hairline rules when separating tabular guest rosters or seating tiers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Margin scales down to `2rem`. Complex dual-column inspector trays collapse into tabbed segments.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Outer margins collapse to `1.25rem`, with tight gutters (`0.75rem`) to maximize screen real estate for scanner viewports, digital pass generation, and fast guest search.

### Layout Principles
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Staging:** Content blocks leverage breathing room. High-importance text fields are deliberately offset rather than tightly centered.
- **Tabular Rigor:** Data-heavy operational screens (guest listings, arrival feeds, security check points) prioritize strict horizontal baseline alignment over boxed card enclosures.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, drop glows, and thick skeuomorphic bevels. Visual depth is established through tonal stepping, frosted obsidian glass, and needle-thin luminous edges.

- **Base Floor:** `#0B0B0E` serves as the canvas foundation.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** `#141419` overlaid with a 1px solid stroke (`#2A2A36`). The surface maintains crisp flat geometry.
- **Layer 2 (Overlays, Drawers & Scanners):** `#141419` rendered at 85% opacity with a `24px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), framed with a micro-border of `#C5A059` at 25% opacity.
- **Luminous Edge Highlights:** Rather than diffused outer shadows, floating elements (e.g., VIP passes, modal sheets) feature an ultra-fine inset highlight: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- **Light Scanner Mode:** When switched to high-contrast scanner/admin mode (`#FAF9F6`), depth is achieved purely through 1px architectural hairline strokes (`#E2E0D8`) and stark contrast rather than shadow emulation.

## Shapes

The primary structural language is uncompromisingly sharp (`roundedness: 0`), reflecting high-fashion tailoring, museum plinths, and geometric invitations.

- **Architectural Edges:** Cards, data tables, input fields, visual containers, and standard button triggers have precise 90-degree squared corners (`0px` radius). This creates an authoritative, structured baseline.
- **Intentional Contrast — Gold Foil Badges:** The singular exception is pill-shaped badges (`border-radius: 9999px`) applied strictly to VIP indicators, credential status tags, and seating row markers. This contrast gives status indicators the look and feel of die-cut metallic seals or luxury wristbands against a crisp rectangular layout.

## Components

### Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Editorial Action:** Squared corners (`0px`), solid champagne gold fill (`#D4AF37`), pure obsidian text (`#0B0B0E`), styled with `label-caps-editorial` in bold tracking. Hover state transitions seamlessly to antique gold (`#C5A059`).
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent background with a 1px hairline border (`#D4AF37` or `#70707B`). Text in ivory (`#FAF9F6`). Hover induces a gold hairline shift and an obsidian surface highlight (`#1E1E26`).
- **Admin/Destructive Trigger:** Deep charcoal background with razor-thin vintage rouge stroke (`#8E2A2A`) and ivory label.

### VIP Badges & Pill Chips
- **Gold Foil Status Pills:** Curated pill silhouette (`rounded-full`, height 24px-28px). Obsidian background encased in a 1px metallic gold border (`#D4AF37`) with gilded micro-caps text (`#D4AF37`).
- **Backstage / Tier Chips:** Ivory background (`#FAF9F6`) with stark black typography for maximum scan visibility during low-light runway entries.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Underline & Minimal Boxed Variants:** Transparent background with a single bottom rule (`1px solid #2A2A36`) or crisp 4-sided sharp border.
- **Active / Focus:** Transitions the bottom rule to 1px champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) with zero default browser outline ring. Placeholder text styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans` at 300 weight (`#70707B`).

### Tabular Guest Rosters & Lists
- **Data Architecture:** Zero enclosed container cards for lists. Rows are delineated solely by horizontal 1px hairline rules (`#2A2A36`).
- **Typography & Interaction:** Guest names set in `title-editorial` (`Playfair Display`), metadata (Affiliation, Runway Tier, Check-in status) aligned along strict vertical columns set in `mono-data`. Row hover activates a soft obsidian tint (`#141419`).

### Seating & VIP Pass Cards
- **The Pass Card:** Ratio 1:1.6 (portrait cardstock proportion). Obsidian background (`#141419`), 1px gold perimeter border, watermarked serif monogram or collection season stamp. Contains barcode/QR module embedded on a high-contrast warm ivory background cutout for immediate optical scanner acquisition.
- **Seating Block Indicator:** Sharp square tiles representing runway bench zones (Zone A, Front Row, Salon Privé) featuring active gold selection outlines and crisp seat indices.
